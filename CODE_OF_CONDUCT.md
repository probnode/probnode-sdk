# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: we pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone. Be respectful and constructive, accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for
the people using and building the project. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and
publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to
**hello@probnode.com**. All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
privacy of the reporter is respected. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits,
code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and may ban
contributors temporarily or permanently.

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
